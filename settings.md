# My settings

Every agent reads this file before it does anything. Change the bulleted values under this heading to your own before your first run. They start filled in for the sample job seeker (Morgan Ellis, a made-up Senior Data Analyst in the US). Job hunting in India? Copy the India example further down over them.

Agents: only the bulleted values right under this heading are settings. The India example and the tips are reference text.

- **Country:** United States
- **Timezone:** America/Denver
- **City and work style:** Denver, Colorado · remote (US) or hybrid in the Denver area
- **Currency and pay basis:** USD, base salary per year (not bonus, equity or total compensation)
- **Salary floor (Job Scout skips listed pay below this):** $130,000 base
- **Salary target (Offer Coach negotiates toward this):** $145,000 base
- **Notice period at my current job:** 2 weeks
- **Resume paper and length:** US Letter, 1 page
- **Cover letter:** yes, under 250 words
- **Report job scams to:** reportfraud.ftc.gov
- **Roles I don't want:** roles that need production machine learning ownership, sales roles

## India example

```
- Country: India
- Timezone: Asia/Kolkata
- City and work style: Bengaluru · hybrid in Bengaluru or remote (India)
- Currency and pay basis: INR, fixed pay per year in LPA (not total CTC)
- Salary floor (Job Scout skips listed pay below this): ₹28 LPA fixed
- Salary target (Offer Coach negotiates toward this): ₹35 LPA fixed
- Notice period at my current job: 60 days
- Resume paper and length: A4, up to 2 pages, no photo or date of birth
- Cover letter: optional, a short email note instead
- Report job scams to: cybercrime.gov.in or the 1930 helpline
- Roles I don't want: bond or deposit-based jobs, sales-only roles
```

Tips for India:
- Say which pay you mean. **Fixed** is your guaranteed monthly salary × 12. **CTC** also adds variable pay, joining bonus, employer PF, gratuity and insurance, so it's always bigger than what you take home. The agents compare your floor and target on the basis you write here.
- Many Indian listings don't show pay. Job Scout keeps them and marks pay as unverified.
- Job boards that need a sign-in can't be read. Company career pages work best, so put the companies you like in `wins.md` under "What I want next".
- Add your notice period. The Offer Coach checks the joining date against it.
